How the analysis works

Corrected input
The analyzer format uses the QC Layer “Corrected” column. For tidy CSV, response values are used exactly as entered. All concentrations are in µM. Files are parsed locally without executing workbook instructions.
Activity definition
Cmax strictly above the adjustable cutoff is active. When available, the matching parameter export’s “Activity (cmax)” is used. With corrected wells only, Cmax is the maximum observed dose mean and is labeled accordingly. These definitions can produce different hit lists.
EC50 ranges and combined criteria
The ranges are ≤1, >1 to ≤3, >3 to ≤10, and >10 µM. Choosing one range requires both Cmax above the cutoff and a reported EC50 assignable to that range. “All ranges” uses Cmax alone. Independent refits are not substituted for missing reported EC50. Qualified values are assigned only if their full allowed interval fits a single range: >10 µM is in the >10 range, whereas >3 µM is unresolved. Missing and unresolved values remain distinct. Values within 1 × 10⁻⁷ relative of 1, 3 or 10 are treated as that boundary to accommodate float32 M-to-µM conversion noise; source values in exports are preserved.
Reported parameters
Reported qAC50 values are converted from M to µM. Qualifiers such as “>” are retained. Reported Sinf, S0, R², Valid and QC issues are kept separate from independent refits.
Independent 4PL fit
Unweighted least squares on individual positive-dose wells. The baseline, high-dose plateau, EC50 and Hill slope are estimated. Increasing and decreasing curves are supported. Hill is bounded to 0.1–5; the EC50 search extends two log units beyond the tested doses. These fits do not reproduce the analyzer’s custom constraints.
Model equation
y = S0 + (Sinf − S0) / [1 + (EC50 / x)^Hill]
The EC50 is the midpoint between fitted plateaus, not necessarily the concentration at which the response equals 50 or 120.
Fit limitations
Flags identify out-of-range EC50, R² below 0.70, a slope at its bound, amplitude below 20 response units, extreme plateau extrapolation and non-monotonic dose means. Flags do not remove doses or override your Cmax activity rule.
Replicates and exclusions
Masked or ignored wells are excluded. Zero-dose observations are shown in the dose table but excluded from log-axis fitting. SD reflects variation between wells at each dose; it is not a confidence interval or proof of biological reproducibility.
Raw plate and replicate QC
Raw CV is 100 × sample SD / mean, calculated separately for each exported channel, only with at least two valid measurements and a positive mean. DMSO controls use source-assigned wells in column 22; inhibitory controls use column 23 (without HaloTag per experiment setup). Sample pairs must match compound, target, dose, plate and physical layout. Raw CV and corrected response gaps are distinct measures. Adjustable review flags and plate patterns are descriptive; they do not exclude wells or establish assay acceptance. Two technical wells do not establish biological reproducibility.
PCA compound map
Complete Cmax profiles are standardized separately for each target and decomposed by SVD. Axis labels show variance explained. Compounds with missing target values are excluded from PCA, with the excluded count displayed. Arrows show target loading directions, scaled for display.
Heatmap clustering
Average-linkage hierarchical clustering using Euclidean distances between standardized Cmax profiles. The ordering highlights similar assay profiles; it does not discover chemical scaffolds.
Activity overlap
Compounds are grouped by their exact set of active targets, including a “None” group. Each compound is counted once in the overlap view.
Spearman relationships
Pairwise Spearman rank correlations use compounds tested on both targets. Ties receive average ranks. The pairwise sample size is shown; no p-values or causal interpretations are assigned.
Scope
This is an exploratory browser application, not a validated replacement for Prism. The included Cyclin D1/CypA experiment has two biological replicates (folders 20260902 and 20260904); each is analyzed separately, and each has matched no-inhibitor and +25 µM CypA-inhibitor curves. This is descriptive with n=2: no biological significance tests or confidence intervals are supplied. Use an agreed statistical design and additional biological replicates for inferential comparisons. The selectivity experiment EXP26007301 also retains two separate biological runs (9 Oct and 15 Oct 2026), each with technical duplicates. Select a run for raw QC and individual results, or use Biological replicates for overlays and observed peak comparisons. No biological significance tests are performed.

Upload formats

Use the well-level and optional parameter-level analyzer exports from the same experiment. Upload one biological run at a time. Included biological runs can be compared in their dedicated views and are not pooled as technical wells.

Tidy input columns:

compound,target,concentration_um,response,replicate

One well per row. Optional columns: masked, ignored. Paste CSV or a table copied from Excel with these headers.

Uploaded values are parsed and analyzed inside your browser, with no data upload to a server. Reloading clears the analysis; export results you want to retain. The included dataset uses the original full RMC identifiers.
